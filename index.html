From an Idea to a Policy

Every proposal follows the same open journey

The same six stages, visible to everyone, for every issue on JanNiti — so progress is never a mystery.

Idea

A citizen names a real problem.

Evidence

Documents, data and lived experience are added.

Deliberation

Citizens and experts weigh reasons, not just reactions.

Consensus

Common ground is identified across viewpoints.

Expert Review

Legal and policy experts assess feasibility.

Government Response

The proposal reaches an authority — and its status is tracked.

Deliberation over popularity

We don't just count likes

JanNiti isn't asking how many people liked an idea. It's asking where people actually agree — on the problem, on a solution, on an amendment — so proposals get stronger, not louder.
